Questions, honestly answered

What happens to my data?

Figures appear only anonymised: project type, spec, range and postcode district — never your name, email or full postcode. Your email is used once to send the invite link, then kept off anything public. Ask to be deleted any time and it’s gone.

How do you stop fake numbers?

Sanity ranges on submission, cross-checks against filed planning applications where possible, and obviously-off entries get removed. Early on the book will say how many entries stand behind every figure — small numbers will be labelled small.

Why contribute instead of waiting?

Because the book only exists if people do exactly what you’d want others to do. Contributors get lifetime free access; later it goes paid for trade and data customers, and free forever for the people who built it.

Who’s behind this?

Thomas Jacks, London. This page is the test: if enough real quotes arrive in the next few weeks, the full book gets built. If you have questions before contributing, email thomasalexanderjacks@gmail.com.

Is this a lead-generation site for builders?

No. Nothing here routes you to a builder or sells your details on. The only product is the dataset.
